A smarter way to learn

Small habits can make a difference

Try these practical learning habits and adjust them to your own schedule.

1. Use active recall

Instead of only rereading your notes, close your book and try to explain the topic from memory. Then check what you missed.

2. Break work into sessions

Divide large assignments into smaller tasks. Short, focused sessions can make difficult work feel more manageable.

3. Review at intervals

Return to important material over several days rather than trying to memorize everything in one sitting.

4. Track your progress

Keep track of completed tasks and learning goals. Use your progress to decide what needs more attention.
